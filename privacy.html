Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Application Purpose

Personal Calendar Automation is a personal application designed to integrate Google Calendar with automation tools such as gcalcli and OpenClaw.

2. Google Calendar Data

The application may access calendar events, schedules, event descriptions, and related information according to the permissions authorized by the Google account owner.

3. How Data Is Used

Google Calendar data is used to perform calendar-related tasks requested or configured by the account owner, including viewing and managing schedules.

4. Data Storage and Processing

Authentication credentials and calendar-related processing data may be stored in the environments configured by the account owner.

When AI integrations are enabled, relevant calendar information may be transmitted to the AI providers selected by the account owner to perform requested tasks.

5. Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use. Google Calendar data is not sold or used for advertising. Data may be processed by services configured by the account owner as necessary for requested functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner can remove stored application credentials and related data from the configured environments. Google access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application owner through the support email provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the application's data processing practices change.
